# Product

## Register

product

## Users

JobPilot serves one job seeker operating their own Boss account on a Windows computer. They use it while present at the machine to configure strict job filters, supervise a bounded batch, and take over whenever the platform requires judgment or verification.

## Product Purpose

JobPilot turns repetitive job discovery and first-contact work into a visible, controllable local workflow. Success means eligible jobs are contacted without duplicate messages, ambiguous actions stop safely, and the job seeker can always understand what the system is doing and regain control immediately.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reliable, restrained. The product should feel like a focused local control console: precise without feeling clinical, capable without performing intelligence, and candid about risk and uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid traditional enterprise admin dashboards, neon cyberpunk control rooms, AI gradients, glass effects, oversized vanity metrics, stacked card grids, and decorative “agent thinking” theatrics. Do not make risky automation feel playful or frictionless.

## Design Principles

- Make the next consequential action unmistakable.
- Show system state, limits, and uncertainty before decoration.
- Keep automation bounded and human control continuously available.
- Explain exclusions, pauses, and failures in direct language.
- Earn density through hierarchy rather than nested containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, body-text contrast of at least 4.5:1, non-color status cues, 200% browser zoom, and reduced-motion preferences.
